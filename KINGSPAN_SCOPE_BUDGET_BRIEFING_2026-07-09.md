## 1. Executive Summary

The project is currently in a constrained delivery position. Even if no new feedback tickets were implemented, the remaining planned work already exceeds the remaining contract budget.

Key facts:
- Contract budget: 400 hours (T&M).
- Remaining hours at start of July: 45 hours.
- Remaining baseline work (excluding the 28 new feedback tickets): 120 hours at current realistic velocity.
- Baseline gap without new feedback: 75 hours over budget.
- Full feedback implementation adds another 96 hours.

- Present Kingspan with clear scope scenarios based on their own ticket priorities.
- Pair this with an objective 40-order benchmark exercise next week to quantify current performance and prioritize improvement based on measurable impact.

## 8. 40-Order Benchmark Proposal (Next Week)

Purpose:
- Replace subjective quality discussion with measurable baseline performance.
- Quantify current value delivered.
- Prioritize next fixes based on impact, not perception.

Proposed approach:
1. Run 40 representative sample orders through the current tool.
2. Kingspan labels expected outcomes per order and key fields.
3. Joint review session to score outputs against agreed rubric.
4. Produce benchmark report and prioritized action list.

Suggested metrics:
- Field-level extraction accuracy.
- Line-item completeness.
- Push-ready rate to Boomi/SAP without manual correction.
- Manual touch time per order.
- Defect category distribution by severity.

Deliverables:
- Baseline scorecard.
- Quantified improvement opportunity.
- Priority mapping to Critical, High, Normal, Low backlog.
- Evidence base for go-live decision and phase 2 scope.

- Do not continue with implicit all-in scope without explicit additional hours approval.
- Keep tone collaborative: objective is stable go-live and trusted long-term partnership.
